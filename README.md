# 🌟 Accessible NavBar: Your Smart & Customizable Navigation Assistant 🌟

Accessible NavBar is a highly efficient and fully accessible floating navigation tool designed exclusively for Jieshuo screen reader users. It adds a sleek, compact, and highly customizable navigation bar (Back, Home, Recents) to your screen, significantly enhancing your daily smartphone experience.

## **Key Features:**

* **100% Accessible:** Properly labeled elements with accurate 'Content Descriptions' ensure smooth reading by Jieshuo for every button (Back, Home, Recents, Settings).

* **15 Premium Themes:** Choose from 15 beautiful color palettes including Dark, Blue Ocean, Cyber Neon, Royal Gold, and Midnight Black.

* **Custom Button Layouts:** Easily change the order of your navigation buttons. Choose from 6 different layouts (e.g., Back-Home-Recents or Recents-Back-Home) to match your preference.

* **8 Sound Profiles:** Make every click satisfying with unique sound effects like Classic DTMF, Soft Pop, Mechanical Click, High Alert, and Intercept Ring.

* **Smart Settings Menu:** Includes a dedicated Settings (⚙) button that can be conveniently positioned on either the left or right side of the bar.

* **Advanced In-Built Feedback System:** Directly report bugs or share ideas using the built-in 'Text' or 'Voice' feedback system. It features pause/resume recording support and a secure, background upload process.

* **Foolproof Cancel Logic:** Safely cancel your feedback submission at any time; the background process stops instantly without triggering any false success prompts.

* **Compact & Unobtrusive Design:** The bar perfectly wraps around the buttons, taking up minimal screen space and allowing background touches to pass through effortlessly.

* **Quick Support Links:** Access the developer's YouTube channels, Telegram, WhatsApp Community, and Email directly from a neatly organized grid view inside the settings.

## **How to Use:**

* Simply execute the extension in Jieshuo, and the navigation bar will instantly pop up at the bottom of your screen.

* To close it, you can either run the extension shortcut again or open the Settings menu and tap the "CLOSE NAVIGATION BAR" button at the very top.

* Tap the Settings (⚙) icon to change your theme, button order, or sound profile on the fly without closing the navigation bar.
